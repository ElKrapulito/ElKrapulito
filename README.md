I build production web platforms end-to-end — from React/Next.js interfaces to NestJS APIs, payment integrations, and the infrastructure that keeps them running.

Currently a **Full Stack Developer at Gobernación de Santa Cruz**, where I work on a digital sanitary card platform and the hexagonal payment intermediary behind it — integrating SQL Server/VUT, MongoDB, LDAP, and QR payment gateways.

## Selected impact

- **~17,000** sanitary card applications from **14,700+** unique applicants in 11 weeks.
- **10,000+** QR payments processed (**Bs 730,000+ / ~USD 105k**), median completion under 2 minutes.
- **99.8%** of the payment intermediary's completed production transactions.
- **100%** webhook delivery across **16,000+** status events.
- Built a separate **Next.js admin panel** with Active Directory/LDAP access, audit trails, API-key lifecycle management, and payment reporting.

I care about clean boundaries, idempotency, reconciliation, and systems that fail closed when money is involved.
